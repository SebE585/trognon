# Linux on A7-A11 iPhones: state of the art

Research date: 2026-10-13. Web sources and the GitHub API; nothing reproduced
on hardware yet.

## Bottom line

Device trees for every A7-A11 iPhone are in mainline Linux. They give a console
on the boot framebuffer, buttons, brightness and, on A11 only, internal NVMe.
No Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, audio, camera, GPU, cellular or real display driver
anywhere. A hobbyist bring-up, not a usable phone.

## Mainline device trees

`arch/arm64/boot/dts/apple/` on torvalds/linux master (7.3-rc7 on 2026-10-13):

- s5l8960x: A7 (5s, iPads)
- t7000, t7001: A8, A8X
- s8000, s8003, s8001: A9 (Samsung, TSMC), A9X
- t8010: iPhone 7/7 Plus (d10, d101, d11, d111); t8011: A10X
- t8012: T2 Macs
- t8015: iPhone 8, 8 Plus, X (d20/d201, d21/d211, d22/d221)

Initial bring-up merged in Linux 6.13 (late 2024), posted by Nick Chan on top of
Konrad Dybcio's earlier port.
https://www.phoronix.com/news/Linux-DT-Patches-A7-To-A11,
https://www.phoronix.com/news/Linux-6.13-Better-Apple-Pre-M1

## Who

- Downstream: Hoolock Linux (Nick Chan), a fork of the Asahi kernel and m1n1
  (https://github.com/HoolockLinux). Last kernel commit 2026-09-03, m1n1
  2026-09-16. ~86-107 out-of-tree commits per release, 6.13 to 7.2.
- Upstream: MAINTAINERS "ARM/APPLE MACHINE SUPPORT" = Sven Peter, Janne
  Grunau (Asahi), reviewer Neal Gompa. Patches go through the Asahi SoC tree.

## Boot chain (HoolockLinux/docs, tutorials/SETUP.md)

- Recommended: checkm8 (gaster or checkra1n), then pongoOS, then m1n1 (`idevice`
  branch), then Linux.
- Alternative: patched iBoot loads `m1n1-idevice.macho` (per-device files).
- Two kernel builds: 4K pages (A7/A8), 16K pages (A9+).
- Tethered. Supported firmware iOS 9.0 to 18.0.

## postmarketOS

- Port "Apple Generic iDevice (apple-idevice)" on the Hoolock kernel + m1n1;
  `m1n1-apple-idevice` 1.5.0_git20260208 in v25.12.
  https://pkgs.postmarketos.org/package/v25.12/postmarketos/aarch64/m1n1-apple-idevice
- iPhone X support announced August 2025.

## Project Sandcastle (Corellium)

Released 2020-03-06, iPhone 7/7+ and iPod touch 7, downstream kernel + Android
10. Repo last pushed 2023-03-25, dormant. Loader still reused, e.g.
https://github.com/xenoaitham/pomme (2026-09-29, never booted on hardware by
its own account).

## Not verified

- postmarketOS wiki (bot wall): a snippet rated touch, screen, battery,
  storage "partial" and USB networking working. Unconfirmed: Hoolock does
  not list touch at all.
- Phoronix 6.18 article (403).
- Absence of Wi-Fi/BT/audio/camera/cellular/charging work is inferred from
  their absence in the Hoolock tables, not stated by any source.
- A9/A10 internal storage status.

## Implication for Trognon

The 64-bit kernel work already has a home (Hoolock, then the Asahi tree, then mainline,
with postmarketOS on top). Trognon must **contribute there, not fork**. What nobody
covers: host-free boot, touch, Wi-Fi, battery refurbishment, and 32-bit.
